HPC Co-Design for Africa

A Kenya–West Africa co-design study to improve access to and use of high-performance computing for bioinformatics research. Rather than building new infrastructure, this project co-designs the training, governance, and workflows around what already exists — bridging the gap between researchers who don't know what to ask for and administrators who don't know what they're being asked.

2024 – ongoing Kenya · West Africa BHKi lead

Open Source Software Survey

A community survey exploring how researchers across Africa use, contribute to, and are blocked from engaging with open source software in bioinformatics. The findings inform BHKi's training priorities and advocacy work for open science adoption in the region.

About BHKi

A grassroots hub for African bioinformatics

The Bioinformatics Hub of Kenya initiative (BHKi) is a community of bioinformaticians, data scientists, and life science researchers based in Kenya, working to bridge the gap between well-established and aspiring bioinformaticians through peer training, mentorship, and collaboration.

Our projects emerge from real community needs — gaps we see in our own training, our own research, and our own institutions. Everything we build is open, reusable, and designed to be adapted by communities across Africa.
